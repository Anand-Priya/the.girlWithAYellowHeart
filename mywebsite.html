a little about me

I'm the kind of person who names her plants, keeps every ticket stub, and thinks the best conversations happen at 2am. I started this journal to hold onto the ordinary moments before they blur together — the light on my kitchen wall, a song stuck in my head, a thought I had on the bus.

Yellow because it's the color I reach for on grey days. Heart because, well, I wear mine pretty openly.
